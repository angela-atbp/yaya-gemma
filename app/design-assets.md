1. Core Identity & Foundational AssetsThese are the foundational design tokens that define your brand across all mediums.The Logo Suite: You need more than just one file. A responsive identity requires:Primary Logo: The full version (wordmark + icon) for header navigation and landing pages.Secondary/Stacked Logo: A stacked version for square or vertically constrained layouts.Logomark / Icon: A simplified, standalone symbol.Monochrome Variations: Pure black and pure white (.SVG or transparent .PNG) versions for dark mode, footers, or complex backgrounds.Typography: Define your font stack early. You will need clear licensing and web-safe files (.woff2 for performance) for:Display/Heading Font: A high-personality typeface for marketing headers.Body Font: A highly legible sans-serif or serif for app interfaces, dashboards, and long-form copy.Color Palette Specification: Exact hex codes, RGB, and HSL values for:Primary brand colors (your main visual identity).Semantic colors (Success green, Error red, Warning yellow).Neutral UI scales (a solid range of 5–8 grays/tints for borders, backgrounds, and text hierarchy).2. App-Specific Brand AssetsApp storefronts and user interfaces require highly specific, pixel-perfect dimensions.App Stores & OS PresenceApp Icon (Favicon / Launcher):Web Favicon: .ico or .png versions scaled down to $16 \times 16$ or $32 \times 32$ pixels.Mobile App Icon: A robust vector asset exported into dozens of varying densities for iOS (AppIcon catalogs) and Android (Adaptive Icons with a separate foreground and background layer to support device-native masking).Launch / Splash Screens: The loading screen a user sees when the app opens. Usually minimal—featuring just the logomark centered on a solid brand background or a subtle gradient.Inside the App UIUI Icon Library: A unified icon set (e.g., Lucide, Phosphor, or a custom set) exported as clean, un-styled .svg vectors. They should share identical stroke weights, corner radiuses, and bounding boxes ($24 \times 24$ pixels is standard).Empty State & Onboarding Illustrations: Spot illustrations used when a user has no data yet, finishes a task, or steps through onboarding. Keeping these in a matching illustration style prevents the app from feeling clinical.3. Marketing & Web Presence AssetsWhile your app relies on tight, clean UI assets, your web presence needs high-impact marketing imagery to convert visitors into users.App Store Screenshots: Crisp, high-fidelity mockups of your app in action, layered inside realistic device frames, often overlaid with brief, punchy value-proposition text.Hero Graphics & Product Mockups: High-resolution cutouts or lifestyle compositions showing your application interface on modern laptop or mobile screens.Social Media Kit: Pre-built templates for your launch announcements, feature updates, and community posts.Profiles: Square/circular formats ($500 \times 500$ pixels).Banners: Horizontal headers for platforms like LinkedIn, X, or YouTube.Open Graph (OG) Image: The hidden image attached to your website metadata ($1200 \times 630$ pixels) that populates beautifully whenever someone shares your link via chat apps or social feeds.
