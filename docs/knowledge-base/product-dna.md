Product DNA & Core Constraints
This document establishes the foundational rules, architectural philosophies, and design guardrails for the Recipe PWA. Every feature implementation and AI prompt must respect these rules.

1. Value Proposition & Target Audience
Our app is a high-utility, low-friction Recipe PWA engineered for busy home cooks who hate wasting food.

The Problem: Most recipe apps focus on discovery, forcing users to buy 15 new ingredients for one meal.

Our Solution: "Reverse recipe matching." The app looks at what is already in the user's kitchen and surfaces meals they can make right now, sorted by what is closest to expiring.
